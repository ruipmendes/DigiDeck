Digi Deck grows sideways: three new integrations that make it a control surface for your entire environment, not just your stream.

## Philips Hue (new)

- Bridge auto-discovery via mDNS + Hue's cloud registry (fallback to manual IP).
- **Button-press pairing** — hit the round button on your bridge, click *Link* in Digi Deck within 30 s.
- Scene activation across any room or zone.
- Per-room / per-light ops: toggle, on, off.
- Brightness sliders per room OR per light — drag to set (0-100%), tap to toggle power.
- Live state: tiles light up when the room / light is currently on.

## Home Assistant (new)

If it's smart-home, Home Assistant probably speaks it. One integration covers Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter, Tapo, LIFX, Sonos, cameras, custom automations — dozens of device brands.

- **REST + Long-Lived Access Token** setup. Point Digi Deck at your HA URL, paste a token, done.
- Domain-scoped picker: entity dropdown filters to just lights when the op is `light-toggle`, just media players when the op is `media-play`, etc.
- Ops per domain: `light-{on,off,toggle}`, `switch-{on,off,toggle}`, `scene-activate`, `script-run`, `automation-trigger`, `media-{play,pause,play-pause,next,previous}`, `cover-{open,close,toggle}`.
- **Advanced**: `service-call` for anything HA can do (arbitrary `domain.service` with JSON payload — great for `climate.set_temperature` and friends).
- Sliders for lights (brightness) + media players (volume). Tap to toggle / play-pause.
- Live state polled every 5 s.

## OpenRGB (new)

- Load any of your OpenRGB saved profiles from a tile — one integration covers Aura / iCUE / Mystic Light / Chroma / Fusion / hardware-supported RGB across your whole rig.
- TCP protocol on port 6742 — Digi Deck talks directly to your local OpenRGB server.
- Devices + profiles enumerated live; the action editor's dropdown lists what's actually available.
